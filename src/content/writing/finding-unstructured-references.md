**With Pauline Chavallard.** Accepted and presented at the Legal Information Retrieval workshop at ECIR 2023 in Dublin.

We built an annotation scheme and a dataset of 6,000 paragraphs from French legal documents. The extraction pipeline combines named entity recognition, dependency parsing, BM25, and sentence embeddings for disambiguation. It reached a micro F1-score of 0.82 and a macro F1-score of 0.925.
