This is the beginning of a notebook, not a publishing schedule. It is a place for ideas that need more room than a short post: technical deep dives, notes on films, and thoughts that do not fit either category.

Some entries will live entirely here. Others will point to work published elsewhere. I will write in English or French, depending on the subject and the voice in which it makes the most sense.

The only rule is that a note should contain something worth returning to.
